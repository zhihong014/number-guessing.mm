How to play

  1. The secret number has exactly 4 digits and can start with 0, like 0023.
  2. The secret number stays the same until you guess it or give up.
  3. Enter exactly 4 digits, then press Guess.
  4. Each guess shows how many digits are right and in the right place.
  5. Example: if the secret is 7264, the guess 1234 shows 2 and the guess 2754 shows 1.
  6. Guess all 4 digits to win, or press Give up to end the game and see the number.
  7. Hints cost XP, and a daily challenge gives bonus XP once per day.
