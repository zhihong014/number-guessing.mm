How to play

  1. The secret number has exactly 4 digits and can start with 0, like 0023.
  2. The secret number stays the same until you guess it or give up.
  3. Enter exactly 4 digits, then press Guess.
  4. Each guess shows how many digits are right and in the right place.
  5. Example: if the secret is 7264, the guess 1234 shows 2 and the guess 2754 shows 1.
  6. Guess all 4 digits to win, or press Give up to end the game and see the number.
  7. Hints cost XP, and a daily challenge gives bonus XP once per day.

About

🎮 The game

Numbering Guessing is a 4-digit code-cracking game for one player or two. Every guess tells you how many digits are right and in the right place. Use the clues to crack the secret number!

🆕 Version 1.0 and what’s new

  • Rock Paper Scissors, best-of rounds and live guess histories
  • Quick reactions, rematch and sound effects
  • Levels, streaks, hints, daily challenge and badges in single player
  • Soft background music and volume settings

🔒 Privacy

Your level, XP, badges and settings are stored only in your own browser. In multiplayer, the 4-digit numbers players lock in are saved anonymously (the number, room code, round and host or guest role, never names) to help improve the game.
